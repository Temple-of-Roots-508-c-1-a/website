Sanctuary Mission & Vision

Temple of Roots fosters sanctuaries dedicated to teaching the sacred mysteries for the purpose of acquiring wisdom, healing, and self-mastery. Through spiritual technologies, ancestral knowledge, and paleoscience, we guide individuals on their journey toward divine alignment and self-discovery.

Rooted in Natural Law and the interconnectedness of all life, we are committed to:

  • Facilitating Healing and Renewal — Providing sacred tools and spaces for transformation.
  • Preserving Ancestral Wisdom — Honoring and applying timeless teachings from traditions including IFA (Ile Ase Ajagunmale), Kabbalah, Gnosticism, Taoism, and Traditional Native American practices.
  • Regenerating Community and Earth — Restoring harmony through conscious living and service.

Code of Conduct: Iwa Pele & The Four Agreements

As a nature-based ministry, we believe that divine wisdom and the forces of nature illuminate the path of moral character.

The Pillar of Iwa Pele (Good Character)

Good conduct is manifested through:

  • Love & Compassion — Acting with kindness toward all kin.
  • Honesty & Integrity — Remaining truthful and aligned with your divine purpose.
  • Justice & Modesty — Seeking balance without ego.

The Four Agreements & Natural Law

We subscribe to these functional principles to maintain clarity within our sanctuaries:

  • Be Impeccable with Your Word — Speak with integrity.
  • Don't Take Anything Personally — Others' actions are a projection of their own reality.
  • Don't Make Assumptions — Find the courage to ask questions.
  • Always Do Your Best — Simple effort avoids self-judgment.
  • Mimic Nature — Follow the patterns of the Earth.

Sanctuaries & Earthship Biotecture

Our estates, such as Earthship Atitlán, are living spaces made from tires, bottles, and cans. Designed by world-famous architect Michael Reynolds, these modular homes are built into mountainsides to remain naturally cool while offering epic views.

Sanctuary Guidelines

  • Sacred Time — Observed from 9 PM–8 AM for rest and integration.
  • Zero Waste Living — Guests are responsible for sorting waste into compost, recycling, and cardboard. Use "bottle bricks" (stuffing plastic into bottles) to minimize landfill impact.
  • Water Stewardship — We utilize ozonated, filtered spring water. Please use water mindfully as service can occasionally be interrupted in our remote locations.
  • Liability — Guests stay at our sanctuaries at their own risk and assume liability for any injuries or interactions with local flora and fauna.

Availability: The Texas Earthship sanctuary (Igbadu, Lindale) is rented out for the year and unavailable for guest bookings until June 2027.

Zero Waste Protocol (B2B)

Our for-profit consortium, Zero Waste Advocacy (ZWA), utilizes distributed ledger technology to tokenize waste diversion.

  • Incentivization — We reward the diversion of materials (reusing, upcycling, composting) rather than burning or burying them.
  • Efficiency — We apply IoT sensors to optimize collection frequency, often saving businesses 10–40% on waste expenses.

Legal & Ecclesiastical Status
